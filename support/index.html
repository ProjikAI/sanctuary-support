Common questions

How do I pair my journal?

Open Sanctuary, tap the S on the home page, then tap Pair Journal. Hold the top of your iPhone against the journal's cover until it shows as paired.

How do I lock and unlock my apps?

Tap your paired journal against your iPhone to lock the apps in your current mode. Tap it again to unlock.

How do I choose which apps get locked?

Tap the S, open Settings, then Modes. Pick the apps and categories for each mode. You can also switch modes from the mode name on the home page.

Can I lock apps without my journal?

Yes. Set up a schedule (tap the S, then Schedule) and Sanctuary locks your chosen apps at those times. To end a running schedule early, swipe the page and tap your journal.

The journal isn't being read.

Sanctuary needs an iPhone running iOS 17 or later (iPhone XS / XR or newer). Remove any thick or metal phone case, and hold the top edge of the phone flat against the cover for a second or two.

Locks aren't working.

Sanctuary needs Screen Time access. Go to iPhone Settings, then Screen Time, and check Screen Time is on and Sanctuary is allowed. If you skipped the access prompt, delete and reinstall the app to see it again.

I lost my journal.

Delete and reinstall Sanctuary to remove your locks, then pair your new journal. Deleting the app also removes your modes and schedules.
