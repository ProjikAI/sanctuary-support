Sanctuary App Blocker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Sanctuary App Blocker ("Sanctuary", "the app") is made by Sanctuary Studios. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version: Sanctuary does not collect, store off your device, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no tracking and no analytics.

What stays on your iPhone

To work, Sanctuary saves a few things on your iPhone only:

  • the modes you create, and the apps and categories each mode locks
  • your schedules
  • the ID of the journal you pair (read from the NFC tag in its cover)
  • your lock sessions, used to show "time today"

This information never leaves your iPhone and is not sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

Screen Time

Sanctuary uses Apple's Screen Time framework so you can lock apps on your own phone. When you choose apps to lock, Apple gives Sanctuary an anonymous token, not the names of your apps or what you do in them. Apple keeps these tokens on your device. We cannot see which apps you use or how you use them.

NFC

Sanctuary reads the ID of the NFC tag in your Sanctuary Studios journal to pair it and to lock or unlock your apps. Nothing is written to the tag, and the ID stays on your iPhone.

The Library

The Library links to videos hosted on YouTube, which open in an in-app Safari window. Sanctuary doesn't see what you watch. When you watch a video, YouTube's own privacy policy applies to it.

Children

Sanctuary is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
